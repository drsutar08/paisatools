Why is paying only the minimum due so costly?

If you pay your full credit card bill by the due date, you pay no interest. If you carry a balance, interest is charged at a high monthly rate, often around 3% to 3.75% a month on Indian cards, which is 36% to 45% a year, and GST at 18% is added on top. The minimum due is usually only about 5% of what you owe, so most of it goes on interest and the balance falls very slowly.

Each month: balance = balance + interest × 1.18 − payment Interest = balance × monthly rate. The 1.18 adds 18% GST on the interest. The minimum due is taken as the larger of the minimum % of the balance and ₹200. Banks work out interest daily and the exact rules differ, so treat the result as an estimate.

Worked example

Say you owe ₹1,00,000 on a card charging 3.5% a month, plus 18% GST on the interest. Paying only a 5% minimum due takes 28 yrs 5 mo to clear and costs about ₹3,72,861 in interest and GST. Paying ₹10,000 a month clears it in 1 yr 2 mo for about ₹31,664.

How to get out of card debt faster

  • Pay more than the minimum. Even a fixed amount a little above the minimum cuts the time and the interest sharply.
  • Stop new spending on the card. When you carry a balance, new purchases usually get no interest-free period.
  • Move the debt to a cheaper loan. Converting the balance to an EMI or a personal loan at a lower rate can save a lot, after checking the fees.

Frequently asked questions

How much interest do credit cards charge in India?

Many cards charge around 3% to 3.75% a month on unpaid balances, which is roughly 36% to 45% a year. GST at 18% is added on the interest. Check your statement for your card’s rate.

Do I lose the interest-free period if I pay only part of the bill?

Usually yes. If you do not pay the full amount by the due date, interest is generally charged on the unpaid balance from the transaction dates, and new purchases do not get an interest-free period until you clear the dues.

Is GST charged on credit card interest?

Yes. GST at 18% applies to interest, late payment fees and other charges on your card.

Does paying the minimum due affect my credit score?

Paying at least the minimum by the due date avoids a late payment, which protects your score. But carrying a large balance keeps your credit use high, which can still pull the score down.
