What is PaisaTools?

PaisaTools is a free, independent website of financial calculators and plain-English money guides for people in India. It helps you check the numbers behind everyday money decisions: what a home loan will really cost, which tax regime leaves you with more, how much your salary works out to each month, or how big a SIP you need for a goal.

It is built and maintained by Diptiranjan Sutar. Every calculator shows the formula it uses, a worked example and answers to common questions, and is checked against the official rules from the Income Tax Department, India Post, EPFO and PFRDA. PaisaTools sells no financial products and earns no commissions. More about PaisaTools

Frequently asked questions

Are PaisaTools calculators free to use?

Yes. Every calculator is free, needs no sign-up and has no usage limit.

Is my financial data stored or shared?

No. All calculations run in your browser on your own device. The amounts you enter are never sent to or stored on our servers.

Are the results accurate?

The calculators use standard formulas for EMI, SIP, FD, PPF, tax and similar products, so results are close to what banks and fund houses show. Actual figures can differ slightly because of rounding, fees, compounding dates and rule changes.

Do the tax calculators use the latest rules for India?

They are built around the current slabs, deductions and limits for FY 2026-27, including both the old and new tax regimes. Tax rules change often, so confirm with the Income Tax Department or a qualified professional before filing.

Can I use PaisaTools on my phone or offline?

Yes. The site works on phones, tablets and desktops, and pages you have already visited can open without an internet connection.

Is this financial advice?

No. The results are estimates for information and education only. Please read the disclaimer and consult a registered adviser before making financial decisions.
