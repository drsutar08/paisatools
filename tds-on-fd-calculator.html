When is TDS deducted on FD interest?

A bank deducts TDS when the interest it pays you in a financial year, across all your deposits with it, is more than ₹50,000, or ₹1 lakh if you are a senior citizen. TDS is 10% of the interest, or 20% if the bank does not have your PAN. It is not an extra tax: it is an advance payment that is adjusted against your final tax when you file your return.

TDS for a year = interest for the year × 10% (or 20% without PAN), if that bank’s interest for the year is above the limit Interest is compounded quarterly, as most banks do, and counted year by year from the deposit date; banks actually count by financial year (April to March), so the timing can differ slightly. Once the limit is crossed, TDS applies to the whole year’s interest, not just the part above the limit.

Worked example

Say you put ₹10,00,000 in a 7% FD. In the first year it earns about ₹71,859 of interest. That is above the ₹50,000 limit, so if you are below 60 the bank deducts 10%, about ₹7,186, as TDS. If you are 60 or above, the limit is ₹1 lakh, so no TDS is deducted on this FD alone.

Avoiding TDS the right way

  • Form 121: if your total tax for the year will be nil, give the bank Form 121 at the start of the year and it will not deduct TDS. Form 121 replaced Forms 15G and 15H from 1 April 2026.
  • Keep your PAN with the bank, so TDS is not deducted at the higher 20% rate.
  • Claim it back: if more TDS is cut than your actual tax, you get the difference as a refund when you file your return.
  • Interest is still taxable: staying under the TDS limit does not make the interest tax-free. Add it to your income when you file.

Frequently asked questions

What is the TDS limit on FD interest?

For FY 2026-27, TDS applies when your interest from one bank in a financial year is more than ₹50,000, or more than ₹1 lakh for senior citizens aged 60 or above.

What is the TDS rate on FD interest?

10% of the interest if the bank has your PAN, or 20% if it does not.

How can I stop TDS on my FD?

If your total tax for the year will be nil, submit Form 121 to the bank at the start of the financial year. Form 121 replaced Forms 15G and 15H from 1 April 2026. Giving a false declaration is an offence, so use it only if you qualify.

Does TDS mean I pay tax twice?

No. TDS is credited against your final tax. If your actual tax is lower than the TDS, you get a refund when you file your return; if it is higher, you pay the balance.
