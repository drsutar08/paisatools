How does the Post Office MIS work?

The Post Office Monthly Income Scheme is a government-backed deposit that pays you interest every month. You deposit a lump sum for five years, receive a fixed amount of interest at the end of each month, and get the deposit back at maturity. The government reviews the rate every quarter, but the rate on the day you open the account stays the same for all five years.

Monthly interest = Deposit × rate ÷ 12 Interest is simple, not compounded. It can be credited to your post office savings account. Interest you leave unclaimed earns no extra interest. The deposit comes back after five years.

Worked example

Say you deposit ₹9,00,000 at 7.4% a year. You receive ₹5,550 every month, which is ₹66,600 a year. Over five years that adds up to ₹3,33,000 in interest, and your ₹9,00,000 comes back at maturity.

Key rules

  • Who can invest: any resident individual, alone or jointly with up to two other adults. A guardian can open an account for a minor, and a minor aged 10 or above can open one in their own name.
  • How much: at least ₹1,000, in multiples of ₹1,000. All your MIS deposits together can be up to ₹9 lakh in single accounts, or ₹15 lakh in joint accounts, where each holder has an equal share.
  • Term: five years from the date of deposit.
  • Tax: the interest is taxable at your slab rate, and the deposit does not get a Section 80C (Section 123 in the Income-tax Act, 2025) deduction.
  • Early closure: not allowed in the first year. Between one and three years, 2% of the deposit is deducted; after three years, 1%.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current Post Office MIS interest rate?

7.4% a year for October to December 2026, paid monthly. The government reviews small savings rates every quarter, so check India Post for the latest rate. The rate on the day you open the account applies for its full five years.

How much can I invest in MIS?

At least ₹1,000, in multiples of ₹1,000. The total across all your MIS accounts can be up to ₹9 lakh in single accounts, or up to ₹15 lakh in a joint account of up to three adults, where each holder has an equal share.

Is Post Office MIS interest taxable?

Yes. The interest is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. The deposit does not qualify for a deduction under Section 80C.

Can I close the MIS account early?

Not in the first year. If you close it between one and three years, 2% of the deposit is deducted; after three years, 1%. At maturity after five years, the full deposit is returned.
