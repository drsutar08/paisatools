Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information PaisaTools ("we", "us") collects when you use www.yourdomain.in and how it is used.

Information you enter in calculators

All calculations run in your web browser. The amounts and details you type into our calculators are not sent to us or stored on our servers.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited and the time of your visit. We use this only to keep the site running and secure, and to understand which pages are popular.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners show ads to you based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

Analytics

We may use a privacy-friendly analytics tool to count visits and see which calculators are used most. This information is aggregated and does not identify you personally.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask about, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Since we don't collect calculator inputs, this usually only applies to emails you send us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact
