How does SCSS work?

The Senior Citizens Savings Scheme is a government-backed deposit for people aged 60 and above, available at post offices and authorised banks. You deposit a lump sum for five years and receive interest every quarter. The government reviews the rate each quarter, but the rate on the day you open the account stays fixed for its term.

Quarterly interest = Deposit × rate ÷ 4 Interest is simple, not compounded, and is paid on the first working day of April, July, October and January. The calculator counts full quarters; your first payment may be smaller because it covers only the days since you deposited. Interest you leave unclaimed earns no extra interest. The deposit comes back at maturity.

Worked example

Say you deposit ₹15,00,000 at 8.2% a year. You receive ₹30,750 every quarter, which is ₹1,23,000 a year. Over five years that adds up to ₹6,15,000 in interest, and your ₹15,00,000 comes back at maturity.

Key rules

  • Who can invest: people aged 60 or above; people aged 55 to 60 who have retired on superannuation or voluntary retirement, if they invest within one month of receiving their retirement benefits; and retired defence personnel aged 50 or above.
  • How much: at least ₹1,000, in multiples of ₹1,000, and up to ₹30 lakh in total across all your SCSS accounts.
  • Term: five years. You can extend the account by three years at a time; the extension earns the rate in force when the account matures, which may differ from your original rate.
  • Tax: the deposit qualifies for deduction under Section 80C (Section 123 in the Income-tax Act, 2025) in the old tax regime. The interest is taxable.
  • Early closure: allowed after one year, with 1.5% of the deposit deducted if you close before two years and 1% after that. If you close within the first year, no interest is paid.

Frequently asked questions

What is the current SCSS interest rate?

8.2% a year for October to December 2026. The government reviews small savings rates every quarter, so check India Post for the latest rate. The rate on the day you open the account stays fixed for its five-year term.

Is SCSS interest taxable?

Yes. It is added to your income and taxed at your slab rate. In the old regime, senior citizens can deduct up to ₹50,000 of interest a year under Section 80TTB. TDS is deducted when your yearly interest crosses the TDS limit, unless you submit Form 121 when eligible.

Can I withdraw before five years?

Yes, after the first year. If you close between one and two years, 1.5% of the deposit is deducted; after two years, 1%. If you close within the first year, no interest is paid.

Can a husband and wife each invest ₹30 lakh?

Yes. The ₹30 lakh limit is per person, so each eligible spouse can hold up to ₹30 lakh in their own accounts. A joint account is allowed only with your spouse, and the whole deposit counts towards the first holder’s limit.
